Privacy Policy for Urgent Ring

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Urgent Ring ("the app") is developed by Ammar S. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

  • Phone call state and caller number (READ_PHONE_STATE, and caller ID via Android's Call Screening role) — used only to detect when the same number has called repeatedly, so it can ring through your Silent/Do Not Disturb settings.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — used only to show a caller's saved name on the incoming-call screen, and to let you pick a contact when creating a caller rule.
  • Answering or ending a call (ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS) — used only when you tap Answer or Decline on the app's own incoming-call screen.

Optional: sharing your location after a missed call

This feature is off by default and only works if you turn it on in the app's “Location sharing” settings and add specific phone numbers there. If you do:

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) — when you miss a call from a number you selected, the app reads your current location once. Background access is needed because missed calls happen while the app is closed.
  • Text messages (SEND_SMS) — the app sends that caller a text message containing a map link to your location. Standard carrier SMS charges may apply. The app never reads your text messages.
  • WhatsApp — instead of sending anything itself, the app can show a notification; if you tap it, WhatsApp opens with the message pre-filled and you choose whether to send it.

Your location is sent only to the numbers you selected, at most once per 15 minutes per number, and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is not stored by the app after it is sent. You can turn the feature off or remove a number at any time.

Where that data goes

Nowhere but your device, with one exception that you control. Urgent Ring has no server or backend. Call history, caller rules, and contact lookups are stored only in a local database on your phone. The app contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK, and never transmits your calls, contacts, or numbers anywhere. The only information that leaves your phone is the location message described above, sent by SMS or WhatsApp to the numbers you chose, and only if you turned that feature on.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. We never receive your data. The location message above goes directly from your phone to the person you selected, through your mobile carrier or WhatsApp, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data. You can also clear it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Urgent Ring → Storage → Clear data.

Third-party links

The app's Donate and Help & Feedback buttons open external services (Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, or your email app) outside the app. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
