# Privacy Policy for Urgent Ring

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Urgent Ring ("the app") is developed by Ammar S. ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what the app accesses on your device and how that information is handled.

## What the app accesses

- **Phone call state and caller number** (`READ_PHONE_STATE`, and caller ID via
  Android's Call Screening role) — used only to detect when the same number has
  called repeatedly, so it can ring through your Silent/Do Not Disturb settings.
- **Contacts** (`READ_CONTACTS`) — used only to show a caller's saved name on the
  incoming-call screen, and to let you pick a contact when creating a caller rule.
- **Answering or ending a call** (`ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS`) — used only when you tap
  Answer or Decline on the app's own incoming-call screen.

## Where that data goes

**Nowhere but your device.** Urgent Ring has no server or backend. Call history,
caller rules, and contact lookups are stored only in a local database on your phone.
The app contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK, and never transmits your
calls, contacts, or numbers anywhere.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because none of it ever
leaves your device.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data. You can also clear it at
any time via Android Settings → Apps → Urgent Ring → Storage → Clear data.

## Third-party links

The app's Donate and Help & Feedback buttons open external services (Buy Me a Coffee,
Ko-fi, or your email app) outside the app. Those services have their own privacy
policies, which we don't control.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continuing to use the app after an
update means you accept the revised policy.
